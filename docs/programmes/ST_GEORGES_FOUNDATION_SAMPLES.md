# St George's foundation samples - coaching review draft

Samples for supervised technique practice, not an individualized clearance or a published workout. Use after the existing ASIM warm-up, before fatigue. One round only to start; do not add this on top of another jump circuit. Allow at least 48 hours between jumping sessions; if school days are consecutive, replace the second day's jumps with movement/landing rehearsal until Coach Du adapts the week. Stop for pain, instability or deteriorating landing control. Include warm-up jumps and other school sport when reviewing total load.

| Day | Library movement | Sample amount | Recovery | Easy cue |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | PLY01 Snap Down | 1 x 5 landing rehearsals; hold finish 2 seconds | 45-60 seconds before next station | Stand tall, drop to an athletic stance, knees follow toes. No jump needed. |
| 1 | PLY03 Two-Leg Pogos | 1 x 10 small contacts | 60 seconds | Small bounces, tall body, quiet feet. |
| 1 | PLY02 Broad Jump + Stick | 1 x 3 controlled jumps; reset fully each rep | 10-15 seconds between reps; 60-90 seconds before next station | Jump a comfortable distance, land on both feet, freeze for 2 seconds. |
| 1 | PLY08 Jump Squat + Stick | 1 x 3 small jumps; reset fully each rep | 10-15 seconds between reps; 60-90 seconds after station | Small dip, jump tall, land quietly and hold. |
| 2 | PLY01 Snap Down | 1 x 5 landing rehearsals; hold finish 2 seconds | 45-60 seconds before next station | Drop under control; knees follow toes. |
| 2 | PLY03 Two-Leg Pogos | 1 x 10 small contacts | 60 seconds | Bounce low and stay in the same spot. |
| 2 | PLY06 Forward / Backward Pogos | 1 x 6 total contacts (3 forward/back pairs) | 60-90 seconds | Tiny movements over an imaginary line; stay tall. |
| 2 | PLY02 Broad Jump + Stick | 1 x 3 controlled jumps; reset fully each rep | 10-15 seconds between reps; 60-90 seconds after station | Comfortable distance; balanced, quiet landing; hold for 2 seconds. |

This is 16 jumping landings on Day 1 and 19 on Day 2, excluding five snap-down rehearsals and all warm-up/other sport contacts. These are sample amounts, not universal limits. If the coach cannot obtain controlled bilateral landings, omit the jumps and use the snap-down rehearsal. Weeks 2-4 retain the same sample amounts until athlete readiness and recovery justify a separately approved change. Optional home work stays low-impact; no unsupervised jump circuit.

Workout presentation: exercise name, amount and rest remain visible; athlete Technique tips and coach How to do it expand for cues. Matching video remains an optional inline action. Copy/print retains cues for poor-connectivity use.

Basis: NSCA, Plyometric Exercises (https://www.nsca.com/education/articles/kinetic-select/plyometric-exercises/) and NSCA youth training/long-term athletic development guidance (https://www.nsca.com/about-us/position-statements/youth-training-and-long-term-athletic-development/ltad-youth-resistance-training-and-early-sport-specialization-what-it-all-means/). Exact samples above are a proposed coaching adaptation, not doses quoted from those sources.
